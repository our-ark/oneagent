Recommendation Bot

Inputs: your profile, platform preferences, and product candidates. Outputs: platform briefs, search terms, purchase review cards, and links you choose to share. External platforms control their own feeds; this demo cannot change their algorithms.

Data and execution

Preferences, chats, and review cards stay in this browser and can be exported as JSON. The app does not log in to websites, read private accounts, or pay. Each future integration needs its own authorization and a reviewable action before sending, booking, or paying.
