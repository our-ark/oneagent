# OneAgent Everywhere — Demo video

Duration: 132.5 seconds. 1920 × 1080, 30 fps.

Recorded from the working local demo. Narration uses the macOS Samantha synthetic voice. Bob uses the local deterministic workflow. Purchases are simulated. The desktop segment shows the browser-rendered desktop workspace, which is also used by the Electron app.

## 00:00:00 — ONE AGENT. EVERYWHERE.

Meet Bob. One personal agent, with one continuing conversation. Let's follow a simple shopping decision across your phone, two stores, and your desktop.

## 00:00:09 — 01 / START ON YOUR PHONE

Start on your phone. Ask for comfortable work sneakers, under one hundred and twenty dollars, in size nine. Bob saves your brief and finds two candidates from your connected stores.

## 00:00:28 — 02 / CONTINUE IN DAYFORM

Open Dayform. Bob knows which pair you're viewing, and remembers what matters to you. Ask about comfort for your commute. The answer combines the store's product facts with your preferences.

## 00:00:45 — 03 / COMPARE IN ANOTHER APP

Switch to Stride and ask about the first pair. Bob connects the new selection to the earlier candidate. Day One is the comfort pick. Arc costs more, and exceeds your budget after tax.

## 00:01:04 — 04 / YOUR DECISION. YOUR APPROVAL.

Return to Dayform and ask Bob to order. The store prepares a checkout for one hundred and seven dollars and eighty cents, including demo tax. Nothing is ordered until you approve.

## 00:01:25 — 05 / BACK ON YOUR PHONE

The confirmed receipt is already on your phone. This is a separate interface connected to the same session. You never need to repeat the decision.

## 00:01:36 — 06 / PICK UP ON DESKTOP

Open the desktop workspace and continue the conversation. Bob still has your budget, size, and saved candidates. The same interface also runs in the demo's Electron desktop app.

## 00:01:49 — 07 / ALWAYS ON YOUR TERMS

You control the connections. Budget sharing is optional, and you can revoke a store's access. Your conversation belongs to your personal session, rather than to any one store.

## 00:02:03 — ONE AGENT. ANYWHERE.

One conversation. Context from each app. Control stays with you. This is OneAgent, everywhere. A working demo, with fictional stores and simulated purchases.
